Cueva — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026

Cueva (“we”, “us”) is a movie‑recommendation app that builds a personal “taste fingerprint” from films you like and suggests what to watch next. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Cueva website (cuevapp.com) and the Cueva mobile apps.

Information we collect

  • Account identity. When you sign in, our identity provider (Auth0) gives us your email address and a unique user identifier. We never see or store your password.
  • Taste data. The films you mark as liked/disliked/seen, your derived taste fingerprint, and your region (e.g. “US”), so we can generate recommendations.
  • Basic technical data. Standard request information (such as IP address and device/browser type) processed transiently by our hosting providers to operate and secure the service.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third‑party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To compute your taste fingerprint and produce personalized recommendations.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We share limited data only with vendors that run the service on our behalf:

  • Auth0 (Okta) — authentication and identity.
  • Neon — managed PostgreSQL database that stores your profile and taste data.
  • Render and Cloudflare — application hosting and content delivery.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — film metadata and poster images. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app, which removes your profile, fingerprint, and feedback from our database. You may also email us to request deletion.

Children

Cueva is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app or by email.
